# Notes for AI helpers

Howl is a social app for NC State students to share short posts about campus life. Keep the real app plain until a visual direction is chosen.

Use `explorations/` for numbered design experiments (`01`, `02`, `03`, …); do not change the real app while trying ideas there.

The project owner is a beginner designer who does not use the terminal. Explain technical steps simply and prefer actions they can take in the app or browser.
